HELP / COMMON QUESTIONS

A few things worth
knowing first.

If your question isn’t answered here, send us a message and we’ll help you figure it out.

Who can work with TAJ STUDIO?

Individuals, new businesses, local shops, growing brands and established organisations. Each enquiry is reviewed based on fit and capacity.

What services do you offer?

Website design and development, brand identity, graphic design, logo design, business stationery, social media design, landing pages and website care.

How much does a project cost?

Costs depend on scope, complexity and deliverables. We prepare a quote after understanding requirements.

How long does a project take?

Timing depends on the work involved, feedback and content readiness. A timeline is discussed before a project begins.

Can I request changes?

Revision rounds and what they cover should be agreed in the project scope before work starts.

Do you provide domain and hosting?

These can be discussed as part of a website project. Any third-party costs and account ownership should be clear in the proposal.

How do I start?

Use the project enquiry form. Share what you need, your preferred timeline and useful references.
