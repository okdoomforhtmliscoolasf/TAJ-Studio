THE IMPORTANT DETAILS

Privacy policy.

How this website handles information when you browse or contact TAJ STUDIO.

Last updated: 16 November 2026

1. Who we are

TAJ STUDIO operates this website to present its creative and digital services. For privacy questions, contact tajstudio.inquiry@gmail.com.

2. Information you choose to share

If you use the project enquiry form, the details you enter are placed into a pre-filled WhatsApp message. The form does not send the message automatically. You choose whether to open WhatsApp and send it.

Information you send through WhatsApp, email, phone or social media is handled by the relevant service and may be visible to TAJ STUDIO so we can respond to your enquiry.

3. Information collected by this website

This is a static website. It does not include an account system, payment processing, advertising trackers or a website analytics tool. The website code does not save the project form entries to a database.

The site is hosted through GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical information such as IP address, browser details and request logs to deliver and protect the service. Google Fonts may also receive technical connection information when the font files are requested.

4. Third-party services

Links and actions may take you to WhatsApp, Instagram, email services or other external websites. Those services have their own privacy policies and practices. Review their policies before sharing information with them. TAJ STUDIO does not control third-party services.

5. Cookies and local storage

The website does not intentionally set advertising or analytics cookies and does not use browser local storage to retain enquiry form data. Hosting or linked third-party services may use technologies of their own.

6. How information is used

Information you send directly to TAJ STUDIO is used to understand and respond to your enquiry, discuss a project, provide requested services and maintain business communications.

7. Sharing and retention

TAJ STUDIO does not sell personal information. Information you send is shared only where needed to respond to you, provide a requested service, use the communication provider you selected, or comply with applicable law. Messages may remain in the relevant email or messaging account according to that provider's retention settings and our business needs.

8. Your choices

You can choose not to submit an enquiry and can contact us to ask about personal information you have sent directly to us. Some information may need to be retained where required for legitimate business or legal purposes.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the website or its services change. The latest version and update date will appear on this page.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, email tajstudio.inquiry@gmail.com or call +91 9819088014.
